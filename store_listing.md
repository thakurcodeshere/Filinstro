## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**Understand your audience. Dominate the algorithm.**

Filinstro is your AI-powered Instagram comment intelligence platform. Connect your Instagram account, analyze every comment across all your posts, and get real-time trend intelligence to grow your following faster.

### 🧠 AI Comment Classification
Filinstro automatically classifies every comment into 13 intelligent categories:
- 🌟 **Praise** — Fans who love your work
- 💚 **Appreciate** — Grateful, helpful responses
- 💪 **Motivation** — Inspiring, encouraging comments
- ❓ **Question** — Followers asking for more
- 😂 **Comic** — Funny, relatable reactions
- 😏 **Sarcasm** — Detect ironic responses
- 📚 **Education** — Knowledge-seeking comments
- 💻 **Tech** — Tech-savvy audience signals
- 🏛️ **Politics** — Politically charged comments
- 💬 **Neutral** — Balanced, objective responses
- 🔴 **Hate** — Negative sentiment detection
- ⚠️ **Abuse** — Flag harmful comments instantly
- 📢 **Spam** — Filter out promotional noise

### 📈 Trend & Growth Engine
- Real-time trending hashtags matched to YOUR niche
- Engagement score tracking
- Niche-specific growth tips from top creators
- Content strategy recommendations based on your comment data

### 🔐 Secure & Private
- Demo mode — explore with no account required
- Instagram OAuth for Business/Creator accounts
- Your data never leaves your device

### 💡 Who is Filinstro for?
- Content creators growing their Instagram presence
- Social media managers handling multiple accounts
- Brands tracking audience sentiment
- Anyone who wants to understand what their followers really think
